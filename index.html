About the symposium

Over the past twenty years, members of the Jaramillo group have gone on to positions in academia, national laboratories, industry and startups around the world.

This symposium brings alumni and current members together to present their current research, in sessions organized by the period each speaker was in the lab. The program highlights how the group’s research has developed and opens a discussion of future directions in the field.

The day concludes with a reunion dinner. Partners and families are welcome at the dinner and at Sunday’s optional activities.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a symposium or a reunion?

Both. The daytime program is a scientific symposium with research talks from alumni and current members. The evening is a reunion dinner and reception.

Who is invited?

Everyone who has been part of the Jaramillo group: former and current graduate students, postdocs, visiting researchers, undergraduates and staff, along with close collaborators.

What is a class representative?

Your class is the year you joined the lab, from Year 1 to Year 20. For example, if you joined in the lab’s third year, you are in the Year 3 class. A class representative helps contact members of their class and coordinate their participation. You can volunteer on the registration form.

Can I bring my family?

Yes. Partners and families are welcome at dinner and on Sunday. Please include them in your registration so we can plan numbers.

Do I have to give a talk?

No. Lightning talks are about eight minutes and grouped by era. Speakers from all career paths are welcome, both in and outside of research.

I can’t make it. Can I still take part?

Yes. Short written or video messages from alumni who cannot attend will be shown during the program. Please send them to the organizers.

Is there a registration fee?

To be confirmed. We will post details here once the budget for catering and dinner is set.

I need a letter for travel or a visa.

Email the organizers and we will send a signed invitation letter.
